1. Refund Period

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee from the date of purchase. If you are not satisfied with Link Forge PRO, you may request a full refund within 14 days.

2. How to Request a Refund

Send an email to linkforge.support@gmail.com with:

  • Your license key
  • The email address used for purchase
  • Reason for refund (optional, helps us improve)

3. Refund Processing

Refunds are processed within 7 business days. The amount will be returned to the original payment method. Upon refund, your license key will be deactivated.

4. Exceptions

Refunds are not available after the 14-day period has expired. Refunds are not available for license renewals.
