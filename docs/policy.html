1. Information We Collect

When you purchase a Link Forge PRO license, we collect:

  • Your email address — to deliver the license key
  • Payment amount and plan type — for transaction records

Payment processing is handled by Robokassa. We do not store your credit card details, banking information, or any sensitive financial data on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your email address is used exclusively to deliver your license key after successful payment. We do not:

  • Send marketing or promotional emails
  • Share your data with third parties
  • Sell or rent your personal information

3. License Verification

The Link Forge WordPress plugin periodically sends the following data to our license server (bitcoins-mining.net/link-forge-api/):

  • Your license key
  • Your site domain name

This is solely to verify the validity and status of your PRO license. No other site data, user data, or analytics are transmitted.

4. Data Storage

License data is stored in JSON format on our server. Emails and license keys are retained for the duration of the license validity plus 90 days for support purposes.

5. Cookies

Our landing page uses a single cookie to remember your language preference. No tracking cookies, analytics cookies, or advertising cookies are used.

6. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us. Upon deletion, your license will be deactivated.

7. Contact

For any privacy-related inquiries, please email: linkforge.support@gmail.com
