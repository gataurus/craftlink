Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Link Forge PRO.

How do I activate the PRO license?

After purchase, you'll receive a license key by email within 24 hours. Go to WordPress admin → Link Forge → Settings, paste the key, and click Activate. That's it — all PRO features unlock instantly.

Can I upgrade from monthly to yearly or lifetime?

Absolutely! Purchase any higher plan at any time. Your new license key will replace the old one immediately. The remaining time on your current plan will not be refunded.

Is the license valid for one site or multiple?

One license = one WordPress site (domain). If you need PRO for multiple sites, you can purchase additional licenses. Each site requires its own activation.

How do I get the free version?

Download Link Forge Free directly from the WordPress.org plugin repository or install it from your WordPress admin panel. Free version includes up to 20 links and 20 shortcodes.

What happens when my license expires?

When your license expires, PRO features become unavailable. Your existing links and shortcodes continue to work, but you won't be able to create new ones beyond the free limit until you renew.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes! We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied with Link Forge PRO, email us and we'll refund your purchase — no questions asked. See our Refund Policy for details.

How does link tracking work?

When tracking is enabled, Link Forge records every click with timestamp, IP address, user-agent (browser/device), and referrer URL. You can view click counts in the dashboard and reset counters anytime.

Can I use custom URL prefixes?

Yes! In the free version, you can set one global prefix (e.g., /go/). PRO users can set individual prefixes for each link or even remove the prefix entirely for clean short URLs.
