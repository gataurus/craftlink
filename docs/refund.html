We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee from the date of purchase. If you are not satisfied with Link Forge PRO for any reason, you may request a full refund within 14 days of your purchase.

To request a refund, please send an email to linkforge.support@gmail.com with the following information:

Refunds are not available after the 14-day period has expired. Refunds are not available for license renewals or subsequent purchases of the same product.
