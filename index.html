How It Works

Two simple tools that solve real problems.

🔗

Link Management

You have a long, ugly affiliate link or a complicated URL. Link Forge creates a short, clean version on your own domain. When someone clicks it, they're instantly redirected. Track clicks, protect with a password, or disable anytime — with or without a prefix.

https://shop.com/ref?id=123&utm=abc → yoursite.com/go/sale or yoursite.com/sale
🧱

Shortcode Blocks

Need custom HTML, JavaScript, or CSS on specific pages? Create a named block in Link Forge, paste the shortcode anywhere — posts, pages, widgets. Edit once, updates everywhere. Use the editor button for quick insertion without leaving the post editor.

[forge block="my-code"] → Your HTML/JS/CSS appears here

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I activate PRO?

After purchase, you'll receive a license key by email. Go to WordPress admin → Link Forge → Settings, paste the key, click Activate. Done.

I didn't receive my license key

The email is sent immediately after payment. Please check your spam/junk folder first. If it's not there, contact us at linkforge.support@gmail.com and we'll resend it within 24 hours.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. Purchase a higher plan anytime. The new key replaces the old one immediately.

One license per site?

Yes, one license = one domain. Need more sites? Buy additional licenses.

How do links work?

You create a key (like "sale") and a target URL. The plugin creates yoursite.com/go/sale — anyone who visits it is redirected. Track clicks or protect with a password.

How do shortcodes work?

Create a block with a title and your code. Use [forge block="title"] in any post or page. Edit once — changes everywhere. Or use the editor button for one-click insertion.

What happens when license expires?

PRO features stop. Existing links keep working. You can't create new ones beyond the free limit until renewal.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes, 14-day money-back guarantee. Email us — we'll refund, no questions asked.
